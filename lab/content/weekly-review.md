Every Sunday I open a blank note and write down what happened during the week. Not a diary, just a list of things I finished, started, or gave up on.

Then I pick three things for the next week. Only three.

That's the whole system.

I never copy the old list forward. If something from last week matters, I'll remember it while writing the new one. If I don't, it can wait.
